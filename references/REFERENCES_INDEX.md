## Detailed Notes on Non-PDF Web & Textbook Entries

- **Judea Pearl (2009), *Causality***: The complete work is a 484-page monograph published by Cambridge University Press (ISBN: 978-0521895606). The official epilogue, reflections, and mathematical addenda from Judea Pearl's Cognitive Systems Laboratory at UCLA are downloaded and archived in [09_pearl2009_causality_epilogue_ucla.pdf](file:///d:/cac-blackbox-to-action/references/09_pearl2009_causality_epilogue_ucla.pdf).
- **UCI Repository (`dua2019uci`)**: Canonical citation for the UCI Machine Learning Repository hosting the Adult Income and Statlog German Credit datasets.
- **FICO HELOC (`fico2018heloc`)**: Official competition challenge dataset and evaluation protocol for Home Equity Line of Credit default risk.
